# v0.12.24-alpha dual-screen test checklist

1. Install the APK on the AYN Thor.
2. Open ThorVault and select a Pokémon in the Vault grid.
3. Check whether the lower display shows its name, species, level, nature, origin and move list while the top display stays on the grid.
4. Press the Details action. On a device with a usable Android presentation display, details should stay on the lower display. On a single-screen device, the normal dialog should open.
5. Use the D-pad and confirm button; check that the page no longer gets a gray tap/focus wash and that the mint focus outline remains visible.
6. If no lower panel appears, the AYN firmware may not expose the lower display as a presentation display; collect Android version/firmware details before further changes.

This is experimental and has not been tested on physical AYN Thor hardware in this environment.
