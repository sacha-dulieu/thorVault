# Third-party software

## PKHeX.Core

ThorVault's optional read-only save engine references:

- Package: `PKHeX.Core`
- Version: `26.7.7`
- Project: `kwsch/PKHeX`
- License: GPL-3.0-or-later

ThorVault does not include Nintendo or Pokémon game assets, ROMs, keys, firmware,
or save files. Users provide their own decrypted saves.

The combined engine distribution must remain compliant with PKHeX.Core's GPL
license requirements.


## PokeAPI sprites

ThorVault can request Pokémon sprite images from the public `PokeAPI/sprites`
repository at runtime. The image files are not included in this archive.
Pokémon names, characters, and imagery are trademarks/copyrights of their
respective owners.
